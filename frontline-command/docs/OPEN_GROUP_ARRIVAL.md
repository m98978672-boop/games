# Coordinating a small shared destination

When several infantrymen head for exactly the same open-ground destination, occupancy checks correctly prevent overlapping, but a soldier who stops at the near edge of the ordinary arrival circle can block followers. This change connects the already-default-on `movement_occupancy_v1` to the existing group reservation mechanism in a narrow additional case: 3–5 aligned friendly infantry with the same destination, no contact and no parked members. The group receives distinct points around the target, and moves with normal collision and obstacle handling. No model inference or new neural network is involved.

The open-ground group uses a **50 px** arrival radius rather than the ordinary 34 px, with reservations 45 px from the goal. This is an intentional change in the meaning of arrival, not proof that units reach the old 34 px circle. Before forming the group, each actual slot is checked for traversable terrain, safe resting position, same open side of walls and clear approach to the original goal. Units with radius over 12 px, stationary members, pairs, more than five members, enemy contact, vehicles, disabled occupancy, and goals in buildings do not use the new open-ground extension. Existing group behaviour for buildings is unchanged. Goal changes/contact cancel group state; collision checks still apply. The route is not mathematically guaranteed and a later obstacle can still block a reserved slot.

## Measurements

- `tools/crowd-u-probe.mjs`: synthetic mirrored U-shaped obstacle, real `Game.step`, fixed identical soldier goals, no combat or other AI systems. Across 24 correlated cases (two sides, group size 3/4/5, four seeds), ordinary arrival was 58/96 people, new grouped arrival 96/96. Because the allowed distance changed, an apples-to-apples physical measure is **within 50 px**: 78/96 before and 96/96 after; within 34 px is 58 before and 0 after. Zero observed sampled wall crossings, overlaps or excessive speed. The paired run had 20 cases with more arrivals and no fewer arrivals. Counts and seeds share one geometry and are not independent battles. The same probe for 6/8/10 soldiers showed 52/192 arriving in both versions; the new extension was restricted to at most five after larger groups proved unreliable.
- `tools/crowd-open-realmap.mjs`: actual map and `Game.step`, six selected sites in one region, both directions, 3/5 people and three seeds; fixed goal, unit decisions delayed, other AI systems disabled. Across 72 correlated cases/288 people, **within 50 px with a clear final approach** was 268 before and 285 after. Ordinary arrival flags: 237 before, 281 after; these use different 34/50 px thresholds. There were 42 vs 68 complete groups by those respective flags, no per-case reduction in flag count, and no sampled wall crossing, overlap or speed violation. The results are not representative of all terrain.
- `tools/crowd-order-probe.mjs`: actual Command API, squad and soldier AI, real HQ/city doors, 24 controlled scenes. In both versions 93/96 crossed the door plane and 80/96 ended within 90 px of the objective on a clear side; the new open-ground extension was **not activated**. No change is claimed for these doors. Other autonomous systems and combat were disabled.
- `tools/crowd-battles.mjs`: six paired 120-second normal AI-vs-AI simulations, all systems active. The new group state appeared in 45 once-per-second unit samples, versus zero before; total kills 293 before / 292 after, no completed wars. RNG streams can diverge. These are not proof of reduced crowding, a win-rate improvement, or balance in full battles.
- The full `npm test` passed, including the Node performance test (400/500 units: 13.54/19.01 ms/tick in this run). Chromium loaded the normal config and passed a controlled five-person shared-goal real-map scene for each faction. These are not browser FPS measurements.

This does not solve general congestion, groups of six or more, moving/opposing flows, distinct nearby goals, fights or indoor chokepoints. Some real-map cases still failed to arrive. Group reservations are transient and rebuilt after loading; exact movement replay after a save is not established. The six short battles do not establish an overall improvement. `tests/crowd-open-arrival.mjs` exercises cancellation, disabled occupancy, blocked destinations and parked members as well as the U scene.
